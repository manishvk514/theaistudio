Resurface find what was deleted

Start here: search this computer for surviving copies

For photos that are permanently deleted — gone from the phone and from every bin. Looks in every place this computer may have kept a copy without telling you: thumbnail caches, recycle bin, phone backups, chat apps, sync caches, mailboxes, temp files. Your Pictures and Videos folders are read first as "what I still have".

Also search web-browser caches (finds pictures viewed in Google Photos, WhatsApp Web, iCloud.com — and many unrelated web images)

Memory card, drive or disk image

Reads deleted file records and rebuilds files from unallocated space. Works best on SD cards, USB sticks, cameras and hard disks.

Thorough: also search off sector boundaries (slower)
Raw devices need administrator/root rights. The source is only ever read, never written.

Phone storage or computer folder

Searches trash and recycle folders, thumbnail stores, app caches (WhatsApp, Telegram, Snapchat…) and looks inside databases for embedded pictures. Files that are simply there count as "still existing".

Backup or export from the past

Google Takeout, iPhone/iTunes backup, Snapchat "My Data", Telegram export, an old disk copy, a .zip. Everything in it that no longer exists in your current library is reported as deleted.

What I still have

Your current photo library. Used as the reference for separating deleted from existing — nothing here is ever reported as deleted.

Cloud account

Lists the account's trash and its existing pictures. Deleted items can be downloaded or put back in place.

Google Photos, OneDrive, iCloud, WhatsApp, Snapchat, Telegram

These services give no outside program access to their deleted items, so there is nothing to "connect". What works is their own trash (while it lasts) plus the copies left on devices and in backups — which this app searches.
