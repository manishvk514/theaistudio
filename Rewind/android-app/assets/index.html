Rewind

Bring back deleted photos and videos

Rewind searches this phone by itself: Android's trash (photos and videos you deleted in the last 30 days), compared with what is still in your gallery. Allow photo access once and it starts.

Photos and videos

To list the phone's trash and compare with your gallery. Rewind only reads; nothing leaves the phone.

Connect to Rewind on your Mac

For everything the phone can't reach by itself: iPhone backups, email, Google, Instagram, memory cards. On the Mac open Rewind › Phone › Use on my phone, then type the address it shows. It opens in your browser.

What this phone can and can't bring back
  • Can: anything still in Android's trash (about 30 days), put straight back into your gallery. On Android 7 to 10 it also opens hidden trash folders, preview caches and chat folders.
  • Can't: files that left every trash. Android encrypts each file and wipes freed space, so their bytes are gone for any app. Copies may still exist in your email, chats, backups and accounts: use Rewind on your Mac for those.
  • Left to your Mac: from Android 11, apps can't open other apps' folders or hidden caches. Rewind on the Mac reads those over a USB cable instead, so this phone app never asks for access to all your files.
